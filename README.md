# bunny and carrots

this is a typing game about a bunny and carrots/lettuce falling from the sky! its really simple to play and you only need a keyboard. letters will appear on the screen and you'll get score after pressing the key. carrot awards 1 point while lettuce gives you 2 points. you also have to make sure they don't fall to the ground or you'll lose 1 heart. you have 3 hears in total.
